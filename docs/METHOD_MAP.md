# Method map

The paper compares three observation contracts:

- **Endpoint-rooted serial path:** coherent root response determines the positive Jacobi path.
- **Known labelled tree:** calibrated diagonal storage plus spatially coded profiles resolves branch-label ambiguity.
- **Unknown reciprocal topology and storage:** threshold, shunt, growth, and clamp observations identify the descriptor under the stated ideal contract; a separate constrained estimator evaluates noisy, redundant designs.

Public cardiac morphology supplies graph structure. Mechanical parameters remain effective model quantities. The estimator and graph selection rules are described in the analysis scripts and data provenance.
